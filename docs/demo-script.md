# Three-minute demo

1. Open the PWA at `http://127.0.0.1:5173` on an Android-sized viewport. Choose 罗马 and press 重新规划 to show the step budget, timeline, map, transit source and freshness.
2. Open the arrival content card. Play the narration, then show that the resolver falls back from WebXR to model-viewer/link-only mode when capabilities are unavailable.
3. Generate a 24-hour caregiver link. Exact location is off by default; the link carries itinerary, progress and coarse location scopes.
4. Switch to 贵阳, enable 轮椅或助行器优先 and lower the step budget. Show the unknown-accessibility warning rather than claiming an unknown place is accessible.
5. Long-press SOS for two seconds. Show the ten-second cancellation window, local emergency numbers, and the disclaimer that the feature notifies authorised contacts but does not dispatch emergency services.
